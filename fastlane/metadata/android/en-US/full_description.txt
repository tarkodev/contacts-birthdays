Birthdays helps you remember the people and dates that matter.

Import birthdays and other events from your contacts or calendar, or add them manually. Browse upcoming dates and choose when to be notified.

Features:
- Sort events by date, name, or age
- Set reminders for the day or in advance
- Keep favorites and notes
- See upcoming events in home screen widgets
- Choose a light, dark, or AMOLED black theme
- Back up and restore your events
